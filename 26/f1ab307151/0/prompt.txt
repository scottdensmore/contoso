## Findings

### 1. The reserve de-centres the hero heading, not just the card (moderate — the answer to your question 1)

This is what makes it read as a mistake rather than as intent. `max-sm:pr-8` is on the outer `flex … justify-center` container, so it narrows the *entire* centred column — `&lt;h1&gt;Get in Touch&lt;/h1&gt;` and its subheading included, neither of which has anything to do with the launcher.

Measured, at all three phone widths:

| | h1 centre vs viewport centre | card left / right gutter |
|---|---|---|
| before (`pr-4`) | **0px** | 16 / 16 |
| after (`max-sm:pr-8`) | **−8px** | 16 / 32 |

The card's 16/32 asymmetry alone reads as acceptable — the background is photographic with no reference edges, and at 390 I would not flag it. What tips it is the heading: large centred display type shifted 8px left of the viewport it is centred in is the single most symmetry-sensitive element on the page, and it is the one thing on the page with no reason to move. Compare `after-contact-phone-390.png` against `before-sim-contact-390x844.png` — the heading shift is the difference you notice first, before the card.

**Suggested fix:** scope the reserve to the card rather than the page container. I tested a card-scoped variant in-browser: it produces **identical** clearance (gap 8, focus ring clears by 4) and an **identical** document height (960 at 390, 988 at 360), while restoring `h1Offset` to 0. Note that a naive `max-sm:mr-4` on the `&lt;form&gt;` regresses the gap to 0, and `mr-8` collapses the left gutter to 0, because the form is `w-full max-w-lg` — the width needs handling alongside the margin. So the direction is sound but the exact class needs working out; I am not prescribing one.

If you keep the container approach, it is defensible — just record in the comment that the heading shift is accepted, because right now the comment derives the 32px thoroughly and never mentions that the reserve moves the hero.

## Finding 1 — the new focus-ring guard reads the ring mid-transition (medium, actionable)

`/home/scottdensmore/Developer/scottdensmore/contoso/apps/web/e2e/launcher-safe-area.spec.ts`, the `"the submit button's focus ring clears the launcher too"` test.

The submit button carries `transition-all`. The test presses Tab and evaluates immediately, so `getComputedStyle` returns the transition's *starting* values, not the ring anyone sees. Measured at all three phone widths:

```
                outline            reach   ringGap
immediate       solid 3px / 0px    3       5        &lt;- what the test asserts on
settled (1s)    solid 2px / 2px    4       4        &lt;- the actual ring
```

`3px / 0px / white` are the CSS initial values — `medium` width and `currentcolor`, which on this button is `text-white`. The settled ring is indigo-600 at `outline-2 outline-offset-2`, exactly as `ACTION_FOCUS` declares. So the doc comment's 4px arithmetic in `control-classes.ts` is correct; the *test* is the thing that is 1px off, in the loose direction.

Two consequences:

- The guard has a one-pixel blind window. An inset producing `submitGap = 3` is a genuine defect — settled `ringGap = -1`, ring under the launcher — and the guard passes it at `ringGap = 0`. Your 24px-inset demonstration still failed correctly because it was 3px past the boundary, which is why the demonstration didn't surface this.
- The two vacuity checks (`outlineStyle !== 'none'`, `outlineWidth &gt; 0`) are currently satisfied by the pre-transition state, which is the "ring that means nothing" case they were written to reject. The comment above them anticipates exactly this ("`outlineWidth` reports the user-agent default even when the style is `none`") and then reads at the one moment when that default is what it gets.

Fix: settle the transition before reading — `await page.waitForTimeout(200)` after the Tab, or better, poll until `outlineWidth` is `2px` so the wait is a measurement too. `ringGap` then asserts 4.

Also worth correcting in the PR record: the `focusRingGap = 5` you reported is the transient. The settled figure is 4, so a future change has 4px of headroom, not 5.
