### 1. DEFECT — Shift+Tab from the panel walks straight out of the dialog

`/home/scottdensmore/Developer/scottdensmore/contoso/apps/web/src/components/sidebar.tsx:67-73`

```ts
if (event.shiftKey &amp;&amp; active === first) {
```

The panel itself is the focus target on open (`panelRef.current?.focus()`), and the panel is not in `focusable` — it carries `tabindex="-1"`, which `FOCUSABLE` excludes. So in the drawer's *initial* state `active === panel`, which is neither `first` nor `last`, and neither wrap branch fires. `panel.contains(active)` is true, so the recovery branch does not fire either. The handler returns without `preventDefault()`, and the browser performs its default backward sequential navigation from the panel — to the previous tabbable in document order, which is outside the drawer.

Measured in Chromium against a reconstruction of the rendered DOM (header trigger + Sign In/Sign Up, then backdrop `tabindex=-1`, then the panel, with this exact handler):

```

shift+tab from panel        -&gt; signup      &lt;-- outside the dialog
shift+tab again             -&gt; l2          (recovery branch, one keystroke late)
after clicking heading      -&gt; panel
shift+tab after heading click -&gt; signup
```

Two reachable paths, not one:
- open the drawer by keyboard and press Shift+Tab as the first keystroke;
- click any non-interactive area inside the panel — a section heading, the padding — which focuses the panel (`tabIndex={-1}` makes it the nearest focusable ancestor), then Shift+Tab.

In the real component the escape lands on the header's **Sign Up** link (unauthenticated) or **Sign Out** button (authenticated) — controls sitting under the backdrop. That is the exact failure #306 was filed for, surviving in the backward direction.

```ts
